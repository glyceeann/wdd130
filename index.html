Glycee Ann Bullecer Picar | WDD 130

Hello! I am Glycee Ann Bullecer Picar, a student from the Philippines who is currently based here in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. I am taking WDD 130 because I have always wanted to understand how websites come to life — how a simple idea can turn into something people can see, click, and experience. I find joy in quiet, meaningful details — soft-toned photos, travel keepsakes, small family celebrations, and those moments when I finally understand something I thought was difficult. Learning web development has been like that for me. At first, it felt big and technical, but now I see it as a creative way to share stories and build confidence. Living in Riyadh has taught me to appreciate both tradition and modern growth. From the calm of the desert to the bright lights of the city, there is so much inspiration around me. This website is my starting point, a place where I can practice, document my progress in this course, and slowly create a space that reflects who I am and who I am becoming as a student and future web developer.

Saudi Arabia

Saudi Arabia is a country in the Middle East, located on the Arabian Peninsula. It is bordered by Jordan, Iraq, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, the United Arab Emirates, Oman, and Yemen. It is known for its vast deserts, modern cities, and rich culture and history as the birthplace of Islam. Riyadh, the capital and largest city where I currently live, is a vibrant center for business, culture, and growth, blending traditional heritage with modern architecture.
